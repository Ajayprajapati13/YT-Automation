Every modern AI system runs on a huge amount of hardware behind the scenes. So why does AI need so many GPUs?

A CPU handles a handful of tasks with a few powerful cores. A GPU takes the opposite approach: thousands of simpler cores, built to run in parallel.

Instead of one task at a time, a GPU splits the work into thousands of pieces and processes them all at once, across every core.

That parallel power flows straight into AI. Data moves through GPU memory and compute cores, feeding the model with results in real time.

One GPU isn't enough. Real AI systems multiply that power across dozens, then thousands of GPUs working together.

That is the real reason AI needs massive parallel computing power.
