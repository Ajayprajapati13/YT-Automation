Every modern AI system runs on a surprising amount of computing hardware behind the scenes. Let's break down why GPUs sit at the center of it all.

A GPU is a processor built to run thousands of small calculations at exactly the same time, instead of one after another.

A CPU handles a handful of complex tasks very efficiently. A GPU trades that for massive parallelism across many simpler cores.

Inside a GPU, thousands of compute cores work alongside fast local memory, constantly moving data back and forth to stay busy.

Modern AI models split their workload across many GPUs at once, so millions of requests can be processed in parallel.

That is why AI needs massive parallel computing power.
